ABOUT US

Mesopotamia Service (MPS) is a leading Iraq- based service and supply provider headquartered in Kirkuk, the heart of Iraq’s energy sector. We offer integrated services across oil and gas field support, logistics, and facilities management. Our goal is to eliminate logistical bottlenecks and enhance operational efficiency for our clients in Iraq’s dynamic oil and gas sector.

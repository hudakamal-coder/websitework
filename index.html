About MPS

Mesopotamia Service (MPS) is a leading Iraq- based service and supply provider headquartered in Kirkuk, the heart of Iraq’s energy sector. We offer integrated services across oil and gas field support, logistics, and facilities management. Our goal is to eliminate logistical bottlenecks and enhance operational efficiency for our clients in Iraq’s dynamic oil and gas sector. With deep local expertise, world-class safety practices, and a reliable network, MPS bridges operational needs with efficient, high-impact solutions.

. Vission

MTo become the most trusted, indispensable, and innovative partner for oil and gas companies across Iraq—recognized for our excellence, safety, and strategic support in field operations.

. Mission

MPS delivers high-quality services and supplies tailored to the evolving needs of Iraq’s oil and gas sector. We leverage strong infrastructure, experienced teams, and a client-first approach to ensure operational excellence and sustainable growth for our partners.
